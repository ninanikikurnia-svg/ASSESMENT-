1. A 0.1 kg toy car is released from the peak of a smooth slide with a height of 1.25 m. Determine the velocity of the toy car when it is at a height of 0.45 m above the floor! (g = 10 m/s²).

Solution:

Given: m = 0.1 kg, h₁ = 1.25 m, h₂ = 0.45 m, v₁ = 0

Apply the Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy: EM₁ = EM₂

mgh₁ + ½mv₁² = mgh₂ + ½mv₂²

gh₁ = gh₂ + ½v₂² → v₂² = 2g(h₁ - h₂)

v₂² = 2(10)(1.25 - 0.45) = 20(0.8) = 16

v₂ = 4 m/s

2. A miniature roller coaster train with a mass of 0.05 kg slides from rest from the peak of a track with a height of 0.8 m.

a. Calculate the velocity of the train when it reaches the base of the smooth track!
b. Calculate the kinetic energy at the base and compare its value with the potential energy at the peak!

Solution:

Given: m = 0.05 kg, h = 0.8 m, v₁ = 0

a. Velocity at the base (v₂):

EP at peak = EK at base → mgh = ½mv₂²

v₂ = √(2gh) = √(2 × 10 × 0.8) = √16 = 4 m/s


b. Energy Comparison:

EK at base = ½mv₂² = ½(0.05)(4)² = 0.4 Joule

EP at peak = mgh = (0.05)(10)(0.8) = 0.4 Joule

Conclusion: The kinetic energy at the base is equal to the potential energy at the peak (0.4 Joule), verifying the conservation of mechanical energy.
